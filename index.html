The Magnet System.

Right now, outside your existing list, almost no one knows you exist. Your list hasn't heard from you in months. Your website gets close to no traffic. Your socials sit untouched. The Magnet System fixes all three, starting with the warmest audience first, so clients start finding their way to you instead of the other way around.

01

Revive your email list

We start with the warmest, most obvious channel: the list you already have. It's hand-picked by you, but hasn't been tapped in a long while, and there are likely clients, referral sources, and connections sitting in it that you've forgotten about. We revive it with consistent, value-driven content, so people remember you exist.

02

Turn on LinkedIn

Next, the second-warmest channel. We rebuild your profile so your ideal client recognizes you instantly, then start posting and connecting, putting you in front of people who've never heard of you, until they have. This is where clients come directly from LinkedIn, something that only happens once you're actually visible.

03

Get found on Google and AI

Then we bring in traffic from search. Your website's copy, design, and ongoing optimization are built so Google, Bing, and tools like ChatGPT and Claude can find you when someone searches for a coach in your industry, or for the exact problem you solve.

All three run together, not one at a time, so leads come in from multiple directions at once. You'll see the momentum build, and the best part is none of it needs anything from you.
